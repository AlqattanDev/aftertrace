Game · Crime simulation

AFTERTRACE

One district, two perspectives. Commit the crime, then come back as the detective and find what you left behind.

Persistent evidence Delayed lab tests Evidence-based arrest Phone and desktop

Every object keeps its identity, prints and ballistics across disposal, discovery, transfer and reuse. Take the contract, choose the weapon and the casing, choose the dumpster, the river or a shallow grave. Then walk the same streets as the detective: witnesses, records and labs with real delays, and an arrest that has to be argued from facts. Possession alone never proves an incident. TypeScript and Three.js, generated geometry, no engine.
