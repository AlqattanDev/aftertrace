# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified default for an empty project: TypeScript, Three.js, lightweight browser interface. Vite for local serving.
## Users
A player on a landscape phone browser, with keyboard/mouse development support.
## Product Purpose
A playable single-player crime simulation in one persistent district. Criminal choices create the evidence a detective must later discover.
## Positioning
Objects retain stable identity, prints and ballistics across disposal, discovery, transfer and reuse.
## Capabilities and Constraints
One contract, non-graphic murder, legal/stolen weapon acquisition, casing choice, dumpster/river/burial, NPC witnesses and retrieval/reuse, delayed labs, explicit evidence-based arrest or unsolved ending. Local saving. No accounts, multiplayer, paid services, DNA, driving, elaborate interiors, interrogation AI, courts or prison.
## Brand Commitments
Orthographic isometric modular brick district, warm windows, navy shadows, restrained yellow interaction accents. Actual geometry and walking; no static scene mockup.
## Evidence on Hand
The supplied goal-objective.md is the full brief. Its mentioned reference image was not supplied in this task attachment or sources. Title "AFTERTRACE" and fictional names are implementation decisions delegated by the instruction to make routine decisions and build directly.
## Product Principles
- Evidence survives ownership.
- Detective facts require observation, records or tests.
- Possession alone never proves an incident.
- Keep the human oriented to one objective.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large touch targets, simultaneous joystick/action touches, safe-area spacing, keyboard actions, selectable evidence within a generous radius.
