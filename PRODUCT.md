# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform and stack

Single-player browser game. TypeScript strict, Three.js, Vite, generated geometry and synthesized WebAudio. The showcase build serves from `/aftertrace/play/`.

## Players

People opening a portfolio link, often alone on a portrait phone with little time and no gaming experience. Desktop mouse and keyboard work too.

## Purpose

Create a tiny gallery heist, then investigate the trail you personally left. A case starts with one obvious action, teaches each step through play, and ends with a reveal, a score and a reason to try again.

## Core loop

Choose an entrance, lift the treasure and pick a hiding place. Three short timing actions reward a neat heist. Switch sides in the same gallery, search its props, recover the treasure, then identify the culprit by matching three physical clues to suspect profiles. Incorrect guesses allow another attempt with a score penalty.

## Progress

Visible clue progress, one to three stars, local best results, consecutive first-guess streaks and six treasures unlocked through solved cases. The optional arcade integration submits completed-case scores.

## Presentation

A bright cutaway toy gallery: lilac walls, pale peach stone, deep plum outlines, brass treasures and mint plants. Clear silhouettes, large controls, short instructions, brief chimes and readable feedback. The mystery is about a missing treasure; its people and objects are fictional.

## Accessibility and limits

Portrait-first layout, targets at least 44 pixels high, visible keyboard focus, text labels alongside clue shapes, remembered mute, reduced-motion support and hints. No account, network service, external asset host or paid feature is required to play. Active cases are short; persistent local records are separate from the old district save format.
